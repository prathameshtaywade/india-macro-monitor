Funding conditions and pricing

Daily: the call money rate against the policy repo rate, and RBI's net liquidity position (row F of the daily money-market release: outstanding operations including the day's; negative means RBI is absorbing liquidity). Monthly: lending and deposit rates on fresh business and on the outstanding book, each dated by the month RBI reports it for.

Call money rate minus repo rate, basis points

Trading days only. Source: RBI Money Market Operations (daily press release), row A.I; repo rate in force that day from the policy history.

RBI net liquidity, ₹ lakh crore

Positive = net injection, negative = net absorption. Reported on non-trading days too. Source: RBI Money Market Operations, row F.
